# Binge TV

Free live TV for Android TV, Google TV, Fire TV and Android boxes. Live TV only.

## Sign in

You need a username and password to use Binge TV. The app checks them when it starts, and again when your account reaches its end date. If the check cannot get through, an account that was accepted in the last 7 days keeps working until its end date.

## What it has

- **Live TV**: a wall of channel logos. Pick one to watch.
- **TV Guide**: what is on now and next. Pick a channel from the guide.
- **24/7**: the always-on categories (movies, sitcoms, cartoons, holidays and more).
- **Favorites**: hold OK on a channel to add it.
- **Multi View**: two channels on screen at once.
- **Search**: find a channel by name.
- **Settings**: switch each free channel source on or off, show or hide categories, and choose the countries the TV guide is downloaded for.

## Install

Download [BingeTV-universal.apk](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HazMat77/binge-tv/main/BingeTV-universal.apk) and install it. It runs on 32-bit and 64-bit ARM devices with Android 7 or newer.

The app tells you when a newer version is out.

## Good to know

- The first channel list takes a few minutes to build. After that it opens from what is saved.
- The channels come from free sources on the internet. Some are down or blocked in some countries at times; switch a source off in Settings if you do not want it.
